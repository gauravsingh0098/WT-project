About the department

Building technical and problem-solving skills

The Department of Computer Science provides quality education and develops technical and problem-solving skills among students, preparing them for careers in software development, research, and emerging technologies.

The department maintains dedicated programming, networking, and AI labs, and encourages students to take part in seminars, coding contests, and industry-sponsored projects throughout the academic year.

Graduates go on to roles in software engineering, data science, systems administration, and further postgraduate study.

1. Form Validation — Contact Enquiry Form

Collects name, email, mobile number, preferred visit date, and a message. JavaScript checks for empty required fields, a letters-only name, a valid email pattern, a 10-digit mobile number, a date not in the past, and a message of at least 10 characters. Shows a success or error message and resets on success.

2. Dynamic Greeting

Reads the current hour with new Date().getHours() on page load and displays "Good Morning", "Good Afternoon", or "Good Evening" in a badge above the hero heading.

3. Dynamic Fee Calculation

Tuition (₹45,000), Lab (₹6,000), Library (₹3,000), and Examination (₹1,500) fees are selectable checkboxes. A "change" event listener recalculates the estimated total instantly.

4. Show / Hide Content

The About Department section has a "Read more" link that toggles a hidden CSS class to reveal details on labs, student activities, and graduate outcomes.

5. Dynamic Content / Image Change

A faculty spotlight widget with Previous/Next buttons updates the displayed name, designation, and specialization, cycling through all three faculty members alongside the full directory table.
