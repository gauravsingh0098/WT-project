About Us

Where advanced medicine meets genuine attention

SMS Hospital was built to close the gap between advanced medicine and real, human attention. Our teams work across cardiology, neurology, orthopedics, general medicine, and pediatrics, with equipment and protocols kept current so decisions aren't slowed down by outdated tools.

Whether it's a routine check-up or something that can't wait, the same standard applies — clear communication, careful diagnosis, and a plan you actually understand before you leave.

  • Experienced doctors across five departments
  • Trained nursing and support staff
  • Modern diagnostic and treatment facilities
