About the hospital

A multi-department facility built around one idea: healthcare should feel unhurried, even when it isn't.

  • Experienced doctors across five departments
  • Trained nursing and support staff
  • Modern diagnostic and treatment facilities

Modern facilities, familiar faces

City Care Hospital was built to close the gap between advanced medicine and genuine attention. Our teams work across cardiology, neurology, orthopedics, general medicine, and pediatrics, with equipment and protocols kept current so decisions aren't slowed down by outdated tools.

Whether it's a routine check-up or something that can't wait until Monday, the same standard of care applies — clear communication, careful diagnosis, and a plan you actually understand before you leave.

Implementation summary

Changes made to this website for the JavaScript Practical Assignment. All five required features were implemented directly inside this file — no separate website was created.

No.JavaScript TaskWhere Implemented on the Site
1Form Validation"Book an appointment" section (id="appointment"). Validates Patient Name, Email, Mobile Number, Age, and Appointment Date.
2Dynamic GreetingHero section (id="home"). A badge above the main heading shows a time-based greeting.
3Dynamic Bill / Price Calculation"Estimate your visit cost" section (id="bill-estimate"). Checkboxes for Consultation, Blood Test, and X-Ray update a running total.
4Show / Hide Content"Meet our specialists" section (id="specialists"). A "Read more / Read less" button toggles a doctor's extended details.
5Dynamic Content / Image Change"Our departments" section (id="services"). A spotlight card with Previous/Next buttons cycles through all five departments.

1. Form Validation — Appointment Booking Form

A new form collects patient name, email, mobile number, age, and appointment date. On submit, JavaScript checks that no field is empty, the name contains only letters, the email matches a valid pattern, the mobile number is exactly 10 digits, the age is between 1 and 120, and the date is not in the past. A success or error message is shown, and the form resets on success.

2. Dynamic Greeting

On page load, JavaScript reads the current hour with new Date().getHours() and displays "Good Morning", "Good Afternoon", or "Good Evening" together with a welcome message in a badge at the top of the hero section.

3. Dynamic Bill / Price Calculation

Consultation (₹500), Blood Test (₹300), and X-Ray (₹700) are selectable checkboxes. A "change" event listener recalculates and displays the total instantly whenever a service is checked or unchecked.

4. Show / Hide Content

A specialist doctor profile card includes a "Read more" button that uses a click event to toggle the visibility of additional details (experience, availability, specializations) via a hidden CSS class. The label switches between "Read more" and "Read less".

5. Dynamic Content / Image Change

A department spotlight widget with Previous and Next buttons updates the displayed department name, description, and icon on click, cycling through Cardiology, Neurology, Orthopedics, General Medicine, and Pediatrics.

Files changed
